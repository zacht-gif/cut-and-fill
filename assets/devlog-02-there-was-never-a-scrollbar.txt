Suggested devlog title:  There was never a scrollbar

========================================================================


Cut & Fill is one HTML file with no build step, so the layout has to fit
whatever screen it lands on. I had a rule in mind for when it could not: if
something overflows, the page scrolls, and scrolling is ugly but honest. There
is a comment in the stylesheet saying exactly that.

The rule was wrong, and it had been wrong since launch.

itch serves a browser game inside an iframe, and that iframe carries
scrolling="no". There is no page scroll. Not an awkward one, none. Anything
past the bottom of the frame is not hard to reach, it is gone.

That turns "overflows by 80 pixels" from a blemish into missing controls.

In portrait, opening the levels drawer added about 600 pixels of level picker
underneath the game. At every phone height, tall ones included, the whole
campaign list was unreachable. You could press the button and nothing you
could see would change.

[SHOT]

Worse: on a 360 pixel wide phone, 43 pixels of the d-pad sat below the bottom
of the screen. The down button was not there. On a 344 pixel frame it was 96
pixels, most of the bottom row.

Landscape was fine, and the reason why turned out to be the fix. Held
sideways, the drawer is already its own scrolling panel pinned over the
screen, so it never relied on the page scrolling at all. Portrait does the
same thing now, with the close button pinned on top of it so there is always a
way back out.

Then the board still had to fit, and three things were eating the column. Two
of them are a little embarrassing.

One stat caption is two words. Below 390 pixels wide it wrapped to a second
line, and because the six stat chips are flex siblings that stretch to match
the tallest one, that single label made all six of them 64 pixels tall instead
of 44. Twenty pixels off every phone screen, because one word wrapped.

The four action buttons wrapped onto a second row below 390 pixels. Another 38
pixels.

The levels button had a full-width row of its own, 30 more, on the screens
with none to spare. On a short screen it is a pill in the corner now.

Measured on the worst level in the campaign, before and after:

390 x 556 - overflowed by 25 pixels. Now fits.
360 x 553 - overflowed by 80, with 43 of the d-pad gone. Now fits.
344 x 500 - overflowed by 133, with 96 of the d-pad gone. Now fits.

No rules changed. The levels are the same levels, every par is still the one
the solver proved, and your saves are untouched. The game just fits on a phone
now, and you can actually reach the levels.

The part worth keeping is that the bug was not in the layout. It was in an
assumption about the environment that I never checked, and was confident
enough about to write into a comment explaining my reasoning.
